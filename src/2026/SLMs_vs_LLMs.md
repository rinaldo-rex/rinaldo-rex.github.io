**LLM** : Large Language Models

**SLM** : Small Language Models, but they're still remnants of Large Language models due to the nature of their corpus on training.

**DSLM**: Domain Specific Language models, which are trained exclusively on their particular domains, with better accuracy, easier guardrails, and reasonable maintenance.
